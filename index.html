A few things you might be wondering

How much do the cards cost?

Standard cards start with one free card per business, then 5 for £39 or 10 for £59. Custom branded cards are 5 for £79 or 10 for £139. UK delivery is free.

Is there a subscription or monthly fee?

No. You pay once for the cards you order. There's nothing to sign up to and nothing to cancel.

How does the card work?

Each card has an NFC chip and a QR code, both programmed with your Google review link. A customer taps their phone on the card, or scans the code, and your Google review page opens. There's no app to download.

What happens if someone wants to leave a bad review?

They can. The cards don't only direct happy customers towards Google or prevent unhappy customers from leaving reviews.

The purpose is to help genuine customers leave genuine feedback, good or bad. That's important to us and helps keep things in line with Google's policies on genuine reviews.

What's the catch with the free Review Card?

There isn't one. It's an easy way for you to try the idea in your own business. We send one standard Google Review Card, programmed for your business, free of charge to UK businesses.

How long does delivery take?

Standard cards are posted within 3 working days and custom branded cards within 7 working days, both with free UK delivery.
