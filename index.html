Will my customers get bombarded with messages?

No. That's not what ReviewMore is about. The idea is to make asking easier, not annoy your customers.

Review requests should be short, clear and limited. Where electronic follow-ups are used, we'll configure the process appropriately for your business and include the necessary opt-out handling.

What happens if someone wants to leave a bad review?

They can. ReviewMore doesn't only direct happy customers towards Google or prevent unhappy customers from leaving reviews.

The purpose is to help genuine customers leave genuine feedback, good or bad. That's important to us and helps keep the process in line with Google's policies on genuine reviews.

What happens to my customers' details?

Your customers remain your customers. Their details aren't sold or used by ReviewMore to market our own services to them. Any customer information processed through the service is there to operate the review-request process you've asked us to provide.

We'll explain exactly what information is needed, where it is processed and how the process works when your ReviewMore account is set up.

Is ReviewMore GDPR compliant?

ReviewMore is designed with UK data protection requirements in mind. Where customer details are used to send review requests, only the information needed to provide the agreed service should be processed and the review-request process must be set up appropriately.

We'll explain how the process works before any customer information is used.
