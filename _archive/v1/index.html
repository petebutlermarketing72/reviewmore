FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. We set everything up and run it for you. You just add customers after each job.

How do customers leave a review?

They tap the link in the text or tap your review card. Either one opens your Google review page directly, so there's no searching for your business.

Will my customers get lots of messages?

No. Each customer gets one request and, if they haven't left a review, one reminder. Then the messages stop.

Do you ask every customer, or only happy ones?

Every customer gets asked in the same way. Google doesn't allow businesses to filter out customers who might leave a poor review, and genuine reviews are what build trust.

Am I tied in?

No. You pay monthly with no contract or minimum term, and you can cancel anytime.

Are there any setup fees?

No. The £29 a month covers setup and everything in the plan. Extra and branded tap cards are the only optional purchases.

Can you guarantee I'll rank first on Google?

No, and nobody can honestly promise that. Rankings depend on several factors. Reviews are one of the ones you can do something about, and more of them helps both your visibility and your chances of being chosen.
